# App Store listing translations

Primary listing: `en-US.md`. Localized storefront copy belongs here by App Store locale. App UI language support is maintained separately in the iOS localization catalog.

Target coverage is English plus these 20 widely used App Store languages, and Swedish:

- Spanish (`es-ES`)
- Simplified Chinese (`zh-Hans`)
- Hindi (`hi`)
- French (`fr-FR`)
- Arabic (`ar-SA`)
- Bengali (`bn`)
- Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`)
- Russian (`ru`)
- Urdu (`ur`)
- Indonesian (`id`)
- German (`de-DE`)
- Japanese (`ja`)
- Marathi (`mr`)
- Telugu (`te`)
- Turkish (`tr`)
- Tamil (`ta`)
- Vietnamese (`vi`)
- Korean (`ko`)
- Italian (`it`)
- Persian (`fa`)
- Swedish (`sv-SE`)

The app and store copy currently cover English, Swedish, Spanish, French, German, Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Bengali, Russian, Indonesian, Turkish, Urdu, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Vietnamese, Korean, Italian, and Persian (22 of the requested 22 locales). The listings lead with direct control of compatible legacy Daikin local HTTP adapters and Apple Home climate services. They do not yet advertise MELCloud or Mitsubishi local control, pending verification on physical equipment.

App Store header artwork is available in English (`assets/app-store-header-5244x2950.png`) and Swedish (`assets/app-store-header-sv-5244x2950.png`). The Swedish header is uploaded to the Swedish App Store Connect localization; the English header is used for the English (U.S.) listing.
